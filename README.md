### About Me

I am a Class 9 student currently studying in a government school in Bihar, but my real classroom is the world of computer science and artificial intelligence. I am deeply passionate about pushing the boundaries of machine learning and building robust software architectures.

- **AI & Deep Learning:** Currently researching and experimenting with different types of activation functions for ML models. I spend a lot of time working with PyTorch, custom autograd engines, and studying the core mathematics that power AI.
- **Backend & Infrastructure:** I specialize in building high-performance APIs, microservices, and setting up CI/CD pipelines. 
- **Library Development:** I actively develop Python libraries, optimizing them with C/C++ extensions for maximum performance.
- **App & Web Dev:** Crafting responsive mobile interfaces and dynamic web applications.
